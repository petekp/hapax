A person or thing one particularly dislikes, dreads, or cannot escape; a persistent bane. A **bête noire** is not a passing annoyance but a fixed object of aversion, the thing that reliably darkens one's mood whenever it appears.

> *Deadlines were his bête noire; he could face any critic, but not a calendar.*

## Etymology

From French **bête noire**, literally "black beast." The phrase entered English in the mid-nineteenth century and kept its French spelling, circumflex and all, as a name for the one dread that will not leave.

## Related Words

- **bane** (a cause of persistent harm or misery)
- **anathema** (something detested; originally a formal curse)
- **aversion** (the settled dislike a bête noire provokes)
- **nemesis** (an adversary one cannot overcome)
